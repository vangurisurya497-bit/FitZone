Trainer Workout Tips

Daily advice curated by our elite level professional coaches to accelerate your goals.

Anabolic Window Myth

You don't need to chug a protein shake within 30 minutes of training. Focus on hitting your total daily protein goal and eating high-quality meals balanced throughout the day.

Progressive Overload

To grow, you must challenge your muscles. Track your weights weekly and aim to increase either the weight, reps, or improve your range of motion/tempo in every workout session.

Sleep & Muscle Growth

Muscles don't grow when you lift; they grow while you sleep. Aim for 7-9 hours of deep sleep to trigger human growth hormone (HGH) release and repair damaged muscle fibers.
